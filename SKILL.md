# Personal Agent Decision Fatigue Entropy Allocator Skill

Designed for **OpenClaw**, **Hermes**, and autonomous personal AI agents to protect executive human willpower, quantify cognitive decision complexity, predict default-choice bias (passive status-quo defaults), and preserve peak morning hours for irreversible choices.

## Agent In-Context Capabilities

- **Ego-Depletion Modeling**: Quantifies daily prefrontal executive capacity consumption based on choice complexity, uncertainty, and irreversibility.
- **Default-Choice Bias Prevention**: Flags when exhausted human decision makers are vulnerable to passive concessions or irrational risk avoidance.
- **Stakes-Based Chrono-Routing**: Shields afternoon fatigue windows from Tier 1 irreversible commitments, routing them strictly to 09:00 - 11:30 AM.

## Tools Summary

1. `audit_daily_decision_budget`: Computes cumulative load units, remaining executive capacity %, and bias risk.
2. `optimize_high_stakes_decision_timing`: Classifies choices into Tier 1/2/3 and assigns optimal execution windows.
